Channels

How the channels work

Email
Blogtrottr, a free third-party service, can email you each new item. It shows ads, and every email has an unsubscribe link.
Feed reader
Apps such as Feedly collect updates from many sites in one place. Copy the address and add it to your reader.
Calendar
Subscribe once and new events appear in your calendar by themselves. Google Calendar can take up to a day to show changes.
GitHub discussions
If you have a GitHub account, open the repository, choose Watch, then Custom, and tick Discussions. GitHub then notifies you of every new discussion.
GitHub releases
If you have a GitHub account, open the repository, choose Watch, then Custom, and tick Releases. GitHub then notifies you of every new release.
Stop following

How to stop

You can stop at any time. Each service has its own way:

  • Email: use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email.
  • Feed reader: remove the feed in your reader.
  • Calendar: remove the calendar in your calendar app.
  • GitHub: open the repository and choose Unwatch.

This site does not store anything about you. Email and reader services are run by the companies named here.
