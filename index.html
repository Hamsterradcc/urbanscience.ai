Urban Science × Artificial Intelligence

Studying cities as dynamic human systems.

We develop spatial, computational, and AI methods to understand urban environments, and translate evidence into more equitable, resilient decisions.

Research agenda

Methods grounded in urban questions.

Our work links urban theory, geospatial data, sensing, simulation, and responsible AI. The goal is not AI for its own sake, but stronger evidence about how cities change and whom those changes serve.

01Urban representation & multimodal learningHow cities are encoded from imagery, maps, mobility, and text.→ 02Human-centered sensing & perceptionHow people experience urban environments, measured at scale.→ 03Mobility, accessibility & inequalityWho can reach what, at what cost, and why it differs.→ 04Climate resilience & environmental justiceHow exposure to heat, air, and water risk is distributed.→ 05Urban agents, simulation & decision supportWhen computational agents can inform planning, and how to test them.→
Approach

Measure, explain, translate.

01 · MEASURE

Observe urban environments

Build valid, transparent measures from maps, imagery, sensors, administrative records, and human experience.

02 · EXPLAIN

Identify urban mechanisms

Combine computational models with careful research design to move beyond prediction toward explanation.

03 · TRANSLATE

Support responsible action

Create open evidence and tools that researchers, communities, and public institutions can interrogate and use.
